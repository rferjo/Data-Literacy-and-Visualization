Overview
In today's world, organizations rely on data literacy and visualization skills to make strategic decisions. These decisions impact profits, efficiency, and innovation. For the first part of this project, you will perform exploratory data analysis techniques on provided or chosen data sets to find patterns and trends. For the second part, you will perform explanatory data analysis techniques to present your findings to two separate groups of stakeholders. Your role is to turn raw data into helpful information that supports organizational decision making. This project prepares you for careers in business intelligence, consulting, and data analysis. Learning these skills will improve decision making and help shape the direction of any organization.

Scenario
As a newly hired data analyst, your job is to help your company understand the trends in the available data. To do this, you will need to rely on reputable, publicly available data sources. You’ll be expected to gather and organize this data into a clear and usable format that can reveal patterns in behavior and preferences. From there, you’ll explore the data to uncover meaningful insights and use visualizations to make those insights easy to understand. Your work will support key decision making, so you’ll present your findings to two groups of stakeholders at the company in a way that’s accessible and relevant to each audience.

Directions
For this project, choose at least two data sets from a reputable data source in your field of interest or from one of the data providers listed in Codio and customize the resulting data-driven narrative for two groups of stakeholders.
